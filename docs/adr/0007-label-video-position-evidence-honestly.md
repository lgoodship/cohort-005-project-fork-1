# Explain what Watched measures

Watched is the sum of the furthest recorded video position in each lesson, as defined in [the glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md). Review labels it **Watched (hours)** and explains that seeking ahead increases it and rewatching adds nothing, so the Platform Admin can interpret its limitations without treating it as elapsed viewing time.

## Consequences

- Lesson completion and Watched are separate measures; neither implies the other.
- Watched can inform a usage flag but does not establish actual time spent viewing.
- Retaining Watched evidence preserves what was recorded without making that evidence more precise.
