# Team Licence Refunds are all-or-nothing, with Seats held during review

A Refund returns the full amount paid for a Purchase, so a Team Licence with any claimed Seat cannot be refunded. While a Refund Request is open, its Team Licence's unclaimed Seats cannot be claimed; decline releases them and approval permanently disables them. Approval rechecks that none have been claimed before changing the Refund Request and Seats together.

## Considered Options

- **Allow claims during review**: rejected because another learner could defeat the Team Admin's Refund Request by claiming a Seat before the decision.
- **Return money only for unclaimed Seats**: rejected because partial Refunds require a per-Seat price and complicate the full-amount policy.
