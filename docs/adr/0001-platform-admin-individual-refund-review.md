# Platform Admins decide individual-purchase refund requests

Only Platform Admins may decide refund requests; being a Team Admin alone grants no such authority. This approval workflow handles individual purchases while keeping team-purchase requests visible for separate handling, because a team purchaser may not be a learner and a team refund can affect several seat holders. Payment processing after approval is outside this slice, so an approval records a decision without claiming that money has been returned.

## Consequences

- The authority check applies to the decision action as well as the screen: a Team Admin's direct approval request must be refused.
- A user who is both a Team Admin and a Platform Admin may decide requests through their Platform Admin authority.
- Team requests must be identifiable and cannot use the individual-purchase approval action; this decision does not establish a team-seat revocation policy.
- The distinction between approval, refund issuance, and customer receipt remains explicit in customer language.
