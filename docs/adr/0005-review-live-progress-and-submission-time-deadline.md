# Review live progress but assess the deadline at submission

Refund review shows current completed lessons out of total lessons and their percentage, current video progress in hours, and current purchase age in calendar days, with flags for breached rules. Lesson and video flags use live figures, but the 14-day deadline flag uses the purchase's age when the request was submitted. This deliberately combines current usage evidence with submission-time deadline assessment so a review delay cannot turn an on-time request into a late one.

## Consequences

- A request submitted on day 13 and reviewed on day 17 shows a current purchase age of 17 days but no deadline breach.
- A learner who progresses from 40% at submission to 60% before review is shown at 60%, with the applicable completion flag.
- The screen must distinguish current purchase age from whether submission met the deadline.
- The figures are current review evidence, not a claim that usage at submission has been reconstructed.
