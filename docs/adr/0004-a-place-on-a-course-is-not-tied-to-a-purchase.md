# Enrollment is independent of the Purchase that first granted access

A learner has one Enrollment per course, supported by an Individual Purchase or a claimed Seat. A Refund Request belongs to one specific Purchase, so approval must preserve the Enrollment when another Individual Purchase without an approved Refund Request or a claimed Seat still grants access. Approval does not remove an Instructor's separate authority to manage their course.

## Consequences

The buyer chooses the Purchase for their Refund Request: Individual Purchases from the course page and Team Licences from the team page. Selecting the newest Purchase indiscriminately could refund a Team Licence when the buyer meant their Individual Purchase.
