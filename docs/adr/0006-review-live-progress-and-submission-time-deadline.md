# Review current usage and assess the deadline at submission

Refund Request review shows current lesson completion, Watched in hours, and Purchase age in calendar days. Usage flags reflect current figures, while the 14-day deadline flag uses the Purchase's age when the Refund Request was raised, so a review delay cannot invalidate an on-time submission.

## Consequences

- A Refund Request raised on day 13 and reviewed on day 17 shows a Purchase age of 17 days without a deadline breach.
- A learner who completes 40% of lessons before submission and 60% before review is shown at 60%, with a completion flag.
- The screen distinguishes current Purchase age from submission-time eligibility; current usage is not a reconstruction of usage at submission.
