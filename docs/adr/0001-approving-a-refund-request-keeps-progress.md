# Approving a Refund Request preserves learning history

When a Platform Admin approves a Refund Request, the learner keeps their lesson completion, Watched evidence, quiz attempts, comments and rating. Deleting that history cannot be undone, Watched is evidence for review, and a learner who makes another Individual Purchase or claims a Seat should resume where they left off. Retained learning history without an Enrollment is expected; see [ADR 0004](0004-a-place-on-a-course-is-not-tied-to-a-purchase.md) for when an Enrollment remains.
