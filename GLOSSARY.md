# Course Refunds

The language used for customers' requests for money back on course purchases and the review of those requests.

## Language

**Platform Admin**:
A user with administrative authority across the platform. Only Platform Admins may approve or decline refund requests.
_Avoid_: Admin, administrator (when the scope of authority is unclear)

**Team Admin**:
A user with administrative authority within a team. Being a Team Admin alone does not grant authority to approve or decline refund requests.
_Avoid_: Admin, administrator (when the scope of authority is unclear)

**Individual purchase**:
A distinct purchase granting the purchaser access to a course. A learner may buy the course again after an approved refund request; the new purchase grants a new access entitlement and starts a fresh 14-day refund window without resetting retained course progress or video progress used for refund rule flags.

**Team purchase**:
A purchase granting course seats to a team, whose purchaser need not be a learner. Team-purchase refund requests require separate handling outside the individual-purchase approval workflow.

**Refund request**:
A customer's request for money back on a course purchase. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval or mean money has been returned.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, reversal, cancellation (as synonyms for a refund request)

**Pending review**:
The state of a refund request that has been received and is awaiting a Platform Admin's decision to approve or decline it. It does not mean the request has been approved or money is on its way.
_Avoid_: Pending chargeback, pending reversal

**Approved**:
The state of a refund request that a Platform Admin has agreed to grant, ending the access entitlement from that purchase while preserving the learner's learning history. Approval neither confirms that money has been returned nor removes an independent entitlement or staff authority.
_Avoid_: Refunded, refund issued (as synonyms for approved)

**Refund approval**:
A Platform Admin's one-time decision to grant an individual-purchase refund request and end that purchase's access entitlement. Repeating approval of the same request has no further effect and cannot remove access from a later purchase.

**Refund issued**:
The stage at which the payment service has accepted the refund for processing. This does not confirm that the money has reached the customer's account.
_Avoid_: Money returned, money received, refunded (when receipt has not been confirmed)

**Declined**:
The state of a refund request that a Platform Admin has reviewed and decided not to grant, with a reason provided to the customer. An approved refund that encounters a payment failure is not a declined request.
_Avoid_: Refund failed, payment failed (as synonyms for a declined request)

**Refund processing failed**:
A failure by the payment service to process an approved refund; the approval still stands. The team remains responsible for following up, and the customer does not need to submit a new refund request.
_Avoid_: Declined, rejected (as synonyms for a processing failure)

**Full refund**:
A return of the entire amount the customer actually paid for the course purchase, including any discount applied to that purchase. Later changes to the course price do not change this amount.
_Avoid_: Current course price (as the basis for a full refund)

**Course access**:
A customer's entitlement to use a course, retained while a refund request is pending review and unchanged if the request is declined. Approval ends only the entitlement from the refunded purchase, without erasing learning history or removing access granted by another purchase or staff authority.

**Learning history**:
A learner's retained record of lesson progress, quiz results, and video watch history for a course, independent of any one purchase. This record survives the end of course access and a subsequent repurchase, and the watch history provides evidence for refund review.

**Course progress**:
A learner's current completed lesson count out of the total lessons in a course, also expressed as a percentage. Course progress is distinct from video watch evidence.

**Video progress**:
The sum of the furthest recorded video position reached in each course lesson, expressed in hours. Seeking ahead increases this measure and rewatching does not, so it is evidence of video progress rather than elapsed viewing time.
_Avoid_: Hours watched, time watched (as names for this measure)

**Purchase age**:
The number of calendar days since a course purchase. Refund review shows the current purchase age while assessing the refund deadline using the purchase age when the refund request was submitted.

**Refund review**:
A Platform Admin's assessment of an individual-purchase refund request using live course progress, video progress in hours, and purchase age in days, together with flags for any refund rules the request breaks; team-purchase requests remain visible for separate handling. Rule flags inform the Platform Admin's judgment without automatically blocking approval.

**Refund rule flag**:
An indication that a refund request falls outside a refund policy rule, using live lesson and video progress but assessing the 14-day refund window at submission time; a delay in review does not make an on-time request late. Lesson and video usage breaches do not block submission, including after repurchase, and a flagged request can still be approved by a Platform Admin.
